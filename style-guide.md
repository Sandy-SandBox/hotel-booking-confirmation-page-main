# Front-end Style Guide

## Layout

The designs were created to the following widths:

- Mobile: 375px
- Desktop: 1440px

> 💡 These are just the design sizes. Ensure content is responsive and meets WCAG requirements by testing the full range of screen sizes from 320px to large screens.

## Colors

### Neutral

- Neutral 900: hsl(33, 15%, 15%)
- Neutral 800: hsl(33, 22%, 20%)
- Neutral 700: hsl(32, 20%, 29%)
- Neutral 600: hsl(33, 17%, 41%)
- Neutral 400: hsl(36, 22%, 86%)
- Neutral 200: hsl(30, 24%, 93%)
- Neutral 100: hsl(35, 60%, 96%)
- Neutral 50: hsl(40, 60%, 98%)
- Neutral 0: hsl(0, 0%, 100%)

### Sun

- Sun 500: hsl(42, 83%, 58%)
- Sun 300: hsl(44, 84%, 75%)
- Sun 200: hsl(45, 100%, 74%)
- Sun 50: hsl(41, 100%, 93%)

### Terracotta

- Terracotta 700: hsl(15, 69%, 37%)
- Terracotta 600: hsl(15, 74%, 41%)
- Terracotta 500: hsl(18, 62%, 47%)
- Terracotta 400: hsl(22, 72%, 56%)

### Blue

- Blue 500: hsl(230, 49%, 56%)

### Rose

- Rose 500: hsl(340, 48%, 52%)

## Typography

### Body Copy

- Font size (paragraph): 14px

### Fonts

- Family: [Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces)
  - Weights: 400 (regular and italic)
- Family: [DM Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Sans)
  - Weights: 400, 500, 600
- Family: [DM Mono](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/DM+Mono)
  - Weights: 400, 500
